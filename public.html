Data Management
Your readings are stored privately on this device. Export a CSV as your backup.
Profiles
Each profile keeps its own readings. Use this to test as a different person, or to share a device with someone.
Switching profiles shows only that person's readings. Your readings are always waiting when you switch back.
Forget Me
Permanently deletes your profile, all your readings, and all your settings from this device. The app will return to the welcome screen so someone else can set themselves up fresh.
⚠️ This cannot be undone. Export your readings as a CSV first if you want to keep them.
